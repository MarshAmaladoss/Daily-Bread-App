Saints Marian and James

First Reading: Acts 15:1-6

Some who had come down from Judea were instructing the brothers, "Unless you are circumcised according to the Mosaic practice, you cannot be saved." Because there arose no little dissension and debate by Paul and Barnabas with them...

Responsorial Psalm: Ps 122

R. Let us go rejoicing to the house of the Lord.

Holy Gospel: John 15:1-8

"Jesus said to his disciples: 'I am the true vine, and my Father is the vine grower. He takes away every branch in me that does not bear fruit, and every one that does he prunes so that it bears more fruit... Remain in me, as I remain in you. I am the vine, you are the branches. Whoever remains in me and I in him will bear much fruit, because without me you can do nothing.'"

Daily Prayer

"Lord, help me to remain in You today as a branch on the vine. Through the intercession of the martyrs Saints Marian and James, give me courage to bear fruit for Your glory. Amen."
